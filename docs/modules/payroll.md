# Payroll

Own effective compensation, component/tax classification, periods, rule packs, snapshots, calculation revisions, approval/finalization, payslips, payment export/reconciliation, and amendments.

Monthly permanent/fixed-term resident/nonresident employees, including WNA, are supported in IDR. Contract category does not determine tax status. Components include fixed/variable earnings, bonus, THR, overtime, approved unpaid leave, proration, deductions, and employer/employee contributions. Support gross/net/gross-up.

Versioned statutory policies cover PPh 21 TER/final-period reconciliation, domestic PPh 26, and applicable BPJS bases/caps/eligibility. Treat treaty/tax equalization/daily-piecework/sector incentives as separately supported rule packs. Missing required policy/input blocks calculation. Use BigDecimal with explicit statutory rounding points.

Run: draft -> calculated -> reviewed/approved -> finalized -> payment processing -> paid. Worker checkpoints per bounded batch. Freeze input versions and line-level results; input revisions invalidate approval. Finalized runs change only through referenced amendments. Payment references prevent duplicate accounting.

Screens: compensation, periods, run progress/errors, period comparison, employee detail, approval, payslips, payment batches, reconciliation.

Acceptance: official golden fixtures, resident/nonresident/year-end/start-exit cases, annualization where applicable, TER with THR/bonus, gross-up convergence, BPJS limits, rounding, cancellation/restart, parallel finalization, and historical immutability.

Sources: https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-58-tahun-2023 ; https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-168-tahun-2023 ; https://www.pajak.go.id/id/pph-pasal-26 ; https://www.bpjsketenagakerjaan.go.id/penerima-upah.html . Verify effective regulations and derive reviewed fixtures during implementation.

## Implementation status

Effective company payroll policy, independently managed compensation, immutable history, scoped reads, optimistic updates, and replay are implemented. The pure calculation library includes the reviewed TER tables, resident final-period reconciliation, domestic PPh 26, bounded gross-up/net treatment, separately configured social insurance contributions, standard monthly-wage overtime, explicit-unit proration, and THR service/amount rules. Optional reviewed pay-basis settings are retained in compensation history. Pure monthly composition combines those rules with reviewed workforce/leave inputs and retains line-level results. See [configuration and rules](../payroll-rules.md) for exact inputs, sources, rounding, access, and bounds.

Versioned opening tax history, independent verification, immutable evidence, and bounded scoped reads are implemented.

Period drafts, frozen participant lists, cancellation, exact workforce references, and independently verified monthly input history are implemented. See [period preparation](../payroll-workflows.md).

Durable per-employee calculation, immutable facts/results, lease/checkpoint fencing, bounded explicit recovery, and payroll/leave source cutoffs are implemented. Run detail separates processing completion from failed employee outcomes. Staged aggregate review, frozen maker exclusions, explicit withdrawal, immutable decisions, and bounded resubmission are implemented. Immutable finalization captures the exact approved run/review, issuer labels, and person/month assessments through one fenced publication transaction. Cancellation and bounded retries retain their history. Finalized same-year tax-history chaining preserves the original opening, assessed allowances, withholding, and prior-employer credits; gaps, rehire, and tax-status changes are explicit review failures. Immutable employee/company payslip JSON reads, bounded month pagination, current ownership/permission checks, and owned mobile synchronization are implemented. Independent batch preparation/release, bounded CSV export, confirmed reconciliation, and owned payment-progress synchronization are implemented; see [payroll payments](../payroll-payments.md). Bounded [PDF delivery](../payroll-payslips.md) uses the same finalized snapshot and access checks, with English/Indonesian labels and embedded fonts. The [company policy dashboard](../dashboard.md#payroll-policies) supports dated review, history and versioned edits. The [compensation dashboard](../dashboard.md#employee-compensation) supports minimal employee discovery, dated review, immutable history and independent edits covering earnings, tax, BPJS and calculation conventions. Calculation, review, finalization, payslip and finance payment dashboard workflows are implemented. Guarded amendment preparation and chronological preview are implemented. Versioned amendment drafts, retained calculations and independent staged review are implemented in the API. Ordinary payroll retains exact predecessor assessment revisions. [Revised amendment publication](../payroll-amendments.md), current payment balances, confirmed recoveries, revision-pinned statements/PDFs and transactional employee invalidation are implemented in the API/worker. Dashboard balances, observed positive-delta instructions and revision-pinned PDF downloads are implemented. Confirmed-return entry/history, retained-revision dashboard navigation, current-revision mobile payslips and balances, and amendment inspection, source selection, preview and draft editing are implemented. Dashboard amendment calculation/cancellation commands and independent review submission, staged decisions, withdrawal and retained history are implemented. Dashboard publication review/admission, bounded progress, retained attempts and explicit terminal-job recovery are implemented. Native retained-revision navigation and immutable encrypted history are implemented, including offline selection and revision-pinned PDF saving. Actual-server native acceptance of nonzero revisions and phone delivery remain; automated tests cover historical navigation. A calculated or reviewed run does not authorize payment.
